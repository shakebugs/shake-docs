# Claude Desktop
> Here's how to connect Claude Desktop to your Shake workspace.

## Follow these steps

1. Open Claude Desktop and go to *Settings* → *Connectors*.
2. Click *Add custom connector* — depending on your version, this is the *+* button next to *Connectors*.
3. Enter the following details:
   - Name: *Shake*
   - Remote MCP server URL: *https://mcp.shakebugs.com/mcp*
4. Click *Add*, then *Connect*. Your browser opens the Shake dashboard, where you should click *Authorize*.
5. Back in Claude, open a new chat, click the *+* button and make sure *Shake* is switched on under *Connectors*.

:::note
Shake is a remote connector, so it belongs to your Claude account rather than to one machine. Connect it once here and it is available in Claude on the web and on iOS and Android as well.
:::

Custom connectors work on every Claude plan. Free accounts can add one.

That's it! You can now ask Claude questions about your Shake tickets — [see examples](/docs/mcp/overview.md#prompt-examples).
