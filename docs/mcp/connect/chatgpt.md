# ChatGPT
> Here’s how to connect ChatGPT to your Shake workspace.

## Follow these steps

You must be on a paid ChatGPT plan, as Free users cannot connect to MCP servers.
 1. Open ChatGPT in a browser — this setup cannot be completed through the Mac app or mobile apps.
 2. In the bottom-left corner, click *Your name* → *Settings* → *Apps & Connectors* → select *Advanced settings* → turn on the switch.
 3. Once Developer mode is enabled, click *‹ Back* at the top of the modal to return to the previous screen: <img src="/docs/screens/mcp/chatgpt-shake-mcp-server@2x.png" alt="ChatGPT developer mode" width="400" />
 4. In the top-right corner, click *Create* and enter the following details:
    - Name: *Shake*
    - MCP Server URL: *https://mcp.shakebugs.com/mcp*
    - Authentication: *Oauth*
 5. Confirm that you understand and want to continue
 6. Click *Create* → you will be redirected to your Shake dashboard, where you should click *Authorize*.

That’s it! You can now ask ChatGPT questions about your Shake tickets — [see examples](/docs/mcp/overview.md#prompt-examples).
